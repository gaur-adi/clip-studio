Clip Studio

Live stream → tight, honest Short

A local web app that watches live streams with a rolling buffer, finds moments, plans a real edit (setup → payoff → reaction), renders 1080×1920 Shorts with Hindi/English captions, and checks quality before anything reaches your output folder. Runs on your machines with free tools only.

How a clip is made

  1. Detect – audio jumps, motion and chat spikes in a rolling buffer (no full recording).
  2. Transcribe – Qwen3-ASR with forced alignment on a Mac; word times kept for the whole source.
  3. Plan the edit – an LLM sees the timestamped transcript and returns segments, named scores (hook, clarity, payoff, energy) and verbatim evidence quotes that are checked against the transcript.
  4. Gate – weak hook, clarity or payoff is rejected or sent to review; energy cannot compensate.
  5. Snap and tighten – cuts never land inside a word; a late payoff is trimmed closer to the setup.
  6. Frame – presets: context, gameplay, gameplay + facecam, with phone safe areas.
  7. Caption – words remapped through the same segment map as the cut; measured wrapping; opening-only banner.
  8. Verify – optional vision check of the payoff frames, duplicate registry, quality gate (duration, black/silence, caption bounds, face overlap).

Design principles

Honest scoresNamed scores with reasons; a model's number is never presented as a virality probability.
Fails safeModel down or invalid answer → unscored heuristic plan marked for review, never auto-approved.
Accurate metadataGame and creator come only from verified settings or stream info; no invented launch or permission claims.
Human in the loopNothing uploads or publishes unless you explicitly do it.

Tested on a real clip

On a 40 s controller-disconnect clip the planner kept hook 7 / clarity 8 / payoff 9, saw the payoff arrive 17.6 s into the edit, and tightened it to a 16.5 s cut with the payoff at 7.4 s and no word cut. Quality gate passed. One clip is an anecdote, not a benchmark; retention results are measured only after publishing.

Run it

git clone <this repo>
cd clip-studio
cp .env.example .env     # point at your Ollama host
./run_app.sh

Requires ffmpeg, streamlink, Python 3, Ollama (e.g. gemma4:12b), and a Mac with the Qwen3-ASR environment for speech recognition. Only clip content you have permission to reuse.
