Clip Studio

Paste a YouTube or Twitch link. It finds the best moments and renders vertical Shorts (captions, your commentary, quiet music). Runs only on this Mac.

Long VODs: give a range to go faster (about 1 min per hour of video).




No full recording: only the last ~4 min is held in a rolling buffer (deleted continuously, wiped on Stop). When the AI sees a moment it cuts ~35 s out of the buffer, and only those clips are kept.
Permission gate (rendering is blocked until this is filled in; saved to docs/allowed_creators.json)
